Unknown stays unknown

Values and known masks distinguish Present, Absent and Unknown. Categorical and multi-choice answers are validated against the pinned ontology. Demographics can remain unknown.

Adaptive questions & explanations

Training-only answer frequencies guide approximate information-gain questions. Whole-answer removal measures the actual change in a model match. Neither establishes a medical causal relationship.

Synthetic data, explicit boundaries

DDXPlus English, Figshare article 22687585 version 2: 223 evidences and 49 conditions. Its selected scope chiefly covers cough, sore throat and breathing presentations.

North American demographic assumptions and modified generation priors cannot estimate clinical prevalence or validate this tool for Indian patients. Rare-condition support and profile overlap are disclosed in the evaluation report.

UI results are ranked only. A high full-record benchmark is not sparse-interview clinical accuracy.

Anatomy is navigation

The optional human-atlas viewer is based on an adult male BodyParts3D reference. It is independent of model input and is not your scan, a diagnostic model or a representation of every anatomical variation.

No viewer JavaScript or geometry is loaded until you open it. Text regions work without 3D.

Care & privacy

Independent safety prompts cover limited sourced chest-pain and FAST-like symptom criteria. They are engineering-reviewed and have not received independent clinician review. No negative answer or absence of a rule is a safety clearance.

The app does not save symptom answers in a database, localStorage, URL or cookie. Text extraction is local to this server and uses a controlled English phrase list. Hosting infrastructure and browser software have their own logging behavior.
